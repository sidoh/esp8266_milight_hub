# Everyday Household Guide: Physical Remotes & Wall Panels (ESP32 Hub)

A simple, plain-English guide to setting up and using inexpensive 2.4 GHz MiBoxer / Mi-Light remotes and wall panels around the home—so family members, guests, children, and older relatives can control lights, fans, blinds, and smart scenes with a single button press, without needing a phone or voice assistant.

## Why Physical Remotes Make Everyday Life Easier
* **No phone required:** Walk in, press a button, and lights or room devices respond immediately—no unlocking a phone, opening an app, or hunting for a screen.
* **No voice needed:** Quiet, reliable, and works when guests are sleeping, when someone prefers not to use voice control, or when quick tactile buttons are easier.
* **Long battery life:** Handheld remotes run for a long time on standard AAA batteries, and wall panels sit right where people naturally reach for a switch.
* **Safer for households:** Includes a **3-Tap Pairing Handshake** when adding a new remote so stray signals don't hijack setup, plus an optional **Idle-Timeout Child Lock** that locks smart actions after a set idle period until a 3-tap unlock code is entered.

## Step 1: Add & Pair Your Physical Remote (3-Tap Handshake)
1. Open the Hub web page and go to **Remotes** (`#/remotes`).
2. Click **+ Detect / Add Remote** in the top-right corner.
3. Follow the 3-step check with the physical remote in your hand:
   * **Step 1:** Press **any button** on your remote so the Hub locks onto that remote's ID.
   * **Step 2:** Press **ON** (or a zone button) to confirm.
   * **Step 3:** Press **OFF** to finish verification.
   *(If you are setting up a remote or adding an ID manually without pressing buttons right then, use **Skip check (manual ID)**.)*
4. Use the **Remote Showroom** to browse large previews, filter by matching protocol, or step through models with **◀ Prev / Next ▶**, give the remote a clear room or person name (such as *Bedroom Remote* or *Hallway Panel*), and click **Save Remote**.

## Step 2: Set Up Gestures, Touch Zones & Child Lock
1. Select your remote in **Configured Remotes & Wall Panels** and click **Gestures & Touch Zones**.
2. Choose which button presses you want to use:
   * **Single tap:** Everyday on/off or room control.
   * **Long hold (e.g. holding OFF):** Great for a "Goodnight" or "All Off" scene.
   * **Double / Triple tap:** Optional extra actions if you want more controls without extra buttons.
   * **Segmented Ring / Sliders:** Turn a color wheel or brightness/CCT bar into clear step zones if needed.
3. **Optional Child / Safety Lock:** Turn on **Enable Child Lock**, pick how long the remote can sit idle before auto-locking (for example, 5 minutes), and choose a simple 3-tap unlock sequence (such as **ON → OFF → ON** or **Zone 1 → Zone 2 → Zone 3**). While locked, smart-home triggers are held back until the unlock sequence is pressed.

## Step 3: Single Stick-On Stencils (Flat Panels & Touch Pucks)
* **Single stick-on stencils** are designed for remotes and panels with a **flat, smooth face** where a single overlay sits cleanly without folding over raised rockers, rotary knobs, or physical pushbuttons—covering the **30 flat glass wall panels and flat circular touch pucks** in the catalog (while remotes with knobs, LCDs, rockers, or physical buttons are suited to individual cut stickers later).
* Select a flat panel or touch puck, open **Stencil Studio**, add clear room or appliance labels/icons, and print at **100% actual size** (check the **10 mm × 10 mm** scale box with a ruler before cutting or applying).

## Printable 1-Page Household Button Cheat Sheet

Print this section at **100% actual scale** (A4 or Letter) and stick it on the fridge, hallway cupboard, or bedside drawer so guests, kids, and family members have a quick reference without needing a phone or voice assistant.

| Remote / Panel Name | Room / Location | Single Tap | Long Hold | Double / Triple Tap | Child Lock Unlock (if enabled) |

### Quick Household Reminders
* **Single Stick-On Stencils:** Best for the **30 flat glass wall panels and flat circular touch pucks**—always check the **10 mm x 10 mm** scale square with a ruler before cutting or sticking.
* **Knobs, LCDs, Rockers & Physical Buttons:** Best suited to individual cut stickers later rather than a single stick-on sheet.
* **Child / Safety Lock:** If a remote has been idle past its timeout, smart actions stay safely paused until the 3-tap unlock sequence is pressed.
